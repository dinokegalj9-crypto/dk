The Void

Eigengrau

The warm dark behind closed eyes. You know it. You can't name it.

It is everywhere and nowhere.

Move your cursor — the medium parts around it, like a hand through dust, or water, or the dark itself. Hold still, and the dark gathers; the light slows. The motes are not stars, not snow, not anything you could point to. That is the point.

Scroll, and it stays beneath you.

The Void drifts slower than this text, so the words feel suspended in it rather than printed on a page above it. There is no horizon, no floor, no sense of scale — so the mind cannot decide whether you are looking up, or down, or inward.

Text remains legible because the Void always recedes. Light blooms are rare and faint; the base never brightens past the dark. This paragraph sits at full contrast against it.

Change the light, change the memory.

Below: retint the Void per collection — one accent at a time.
