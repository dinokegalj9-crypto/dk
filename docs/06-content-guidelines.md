# 06 — Content Guidelines

> We never sell. We invite recognition.

## 1. The voice

Intimate, unhurried, certain. We write the way a person speaks when they're telling you
something true and slightly painful — quietly, without flourish, trusting you to feel it.

**Four attributes, always:**

- **Intimate** — second person, close, as if speaking only to you. "You know this room."
- **Unhurried** — short sentences with space around them. Pauses. We let silence do work.
- **Certain** — we don't hedge ("maybe," "kind of," "we think"). We state the feeling plainly
  and let its truth land.
- **Sensory & specific** — concrete detail over abstraction. "The light through a dirty window"
  not "nostalgic vibes."

**We are not:** clever, ironic, hyped, salesy, jargon-heavy, or precious. Never purple prose.
The restraint of the writing *is* the luxury.

## 2. The core technique — describe the state, not the scent

We name what you **become**, not what's in the vial.

- ✗ "Top notes of bergamot and vetiver over a warm amber base."
- ✓ "The last warm afternoon of the year, when you didn't yet know it was the last."

Ingredients exist (people will ask, and honesty matters) but they live in a quiet,
secondary place — a *Composition* note, set small, factual, never the lead (see §6).

## 3. Copy patterns by moment

- **Threshold (the first breath):** one line. A question or a statement that opens a door.
  - *"Some things you didn't lose. You just stopped visiting them."*
  - *"What does it smell like to be seven years old in late summer?"*
- **A fragrance's opening:** the **state**, stated as if you already know it.
  - *"Undertow. The pull of water you can't see, deciding to let it take you."*
- **The emotional body:** 2–4 short paragraphs that *unfold the memory in time*, mirroring how
  the scent itself unfolds (the arc — see [Brand §4](./01-brand-guidelines.md)). Present tense,
  second person, sensory.
- **The quiet decision (commerce):** plain, warm, unpushy.
  - *"Keep it."* / *"Take it with you."* — never "Add to cart," never "Buy now."
- **Confirmation / after:** continue the spell, don't break into receipt-speak.
  - *"It's yours now. It will find you in about three days."*

## 4. Microcopy

Even the smallest words stay in character. The utility moments are where most brands drop the
mask — we don't.

| Function | Avoid | Sensorium |
|----------|-------|-----------|
| Add to cart | "Add to Cart" | "Keep" / "Take it with you" |
| Cart | "Your Cart (2)" | "Kept" |
| Newsletter | "Subscribe for deals!" | "Stay close." / "Let us write to you." |
| 404 | "Page not found" | "This memory hasn't formed yet." |
| Empty state | "No items" | "Nothing here yet — only quiet." |
| Loading | "Loading…" | (none — design it as atmosphere; see [Motion](./04-motion-guidelines.md)) |
| Form error | "Invalid email!" | "That address doesn't seem right — try again." |
| Sold out | "Out of stock" | "Resting. Back soon." |

- **No exclamation marks** in brand voice. Ever.
- **No urgency language** ("Hurry," "Limited," "Only 3 left," countdowns). Forbidden by brand.
- Buttons are verbs of *keeping and continuing*, not *buying and converting*.

## 5. Naming (works with [Brand §5](./01-brand-guidelines.md))

- Fragrance names: 1–3 words, suggestive, never literal or ingredient-based. *Undertow*,
  *The Long Afternoon*, *Threshold*, *After You Left*, *Static*.
- Collections: thresholds of consciousness — *Waking*, *Submerged*, *Residue*, *The Edge of
  Sleep*.
- Never the word "perfume" in primary copy. Prefer *fragrance*, *scent*, or just the name.

## 6. Honesty & the necessary facts

Restraint is not evasion. People deserve real information, given calmly:

- **Composition:** ingredients/notes, listed plainly and small, under a quiet heading
  (*Composition*). Honest, never hidden, never the headline.
- **Practical facts** — size, concentration, longevity, allergens/IFRA, price, shipping,
  returns — are stated clearly and findable. We are atmospheric, not obscure. Withholding
  *feeling* is craft; withholding *facts* is bad faith.
- **Sustainability, sourcing, and claims** must be true and specific. No vague "clean,"
  "natural," or greenwashing. If we can't substantiate it, we don't say it.

## 7. Editorial standards

- **Grammar & punctuation:** impeccable. Em-dashes, true quotes, ellipses (see
  [Typography §6](./05-typography-guidelines.md)). One space after periods.
- **Sentence length:** vary, but lean short. A long sentence earns its length, then a short one
  lands the feeling.
- **Active voice, present tense** for emotional copy; clear plain language for utility.
- **Inclusive & accessible:** never assume gender, body, age, or memory. The states are
  universal-but-specific; write so anyone can find themselves inside them. Avoid idioms that
  don't translate; this brand may live in many languages.
- **Alt text is copy too** — write it with the same care (see [Photography](./07-photography-guidelines.md)).

## 8. The test

> *Read it aloud, quietly. Does it sound like a true thing said to one person — or like marketing?*
